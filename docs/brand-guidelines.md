# Rushi brand guidelines

## Essence

Rushi is a Jaffna vegetarian kitchen rooted in tradition and made for sharing. The identity should feel warm, graceful, and quietly distinctive.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aubergine | `#482A3A` | Wordmark, display headings, footer, selected backgrounds |
| Ivory | `#F8F2E7` | Main page background and light sections |
| Saffron | `#D99A35` | Primary calls to action and small highlights |
| Rose | `#C9806B` | Supporting accents and labels |
| Charcoal | `#292522` | Body copy, prices, high contrast details |

## Type

- Display: Cormorant Garamond, sentence case, with restrained italic emphasis.
- Interface: DM Sans for navigation, dish details, prices, and actions.
- Optional Tamil type: only use after confirming the exact wording and font rendering.

## Voice and copy

Use the core description: “Traditional vegetarian dishes from Jaffna and South India, prepared with care and served with warmth.” Name ingredients clearly, be specific about regional roots, and avoid unsupported superlatives.

## Visuals

Give food photography space and use warm, natural color. Keep ornamental kolam inspired line art secondary to food and type. Maintain readable contrast for labels and controls.
